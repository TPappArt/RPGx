# RPGx Visual Style

Approved visual direction recorded on 15 October 2026.

Use this document with docs/WORLD_OVERVIEW.md. These are approved art directions for the current working race concepts. They do not settle an undecided playable roster, ancestry, culture, or story event.

## Main visual reference

The revised portrait set is the reference for character anatomy, facial rendering, materials, colour and restraint:

## Graphic treatment

- Faceted, low-poly-inspired forms with hand-painted colour and visible broad angular planes.
- Readable silhouettes, expressive faces and restrained detail.
- Colourful, mysterious frontier fantasy with earthy fabrics, weathered leather, dull iron and stone.
- Warm light and quiet cooler shadows. Avoid photorealism, glossy film rendering, anime treatment and elaborate high fantasy decoration.
- Apply the same graphic language to characters, equipment and environments.
- The in-game character budget remains a maximum of 2000 polygons including gear. Painted portrait detail is not permission to increase mesh complexity.

## Armour and clothing

Equipment belongs to the present world's broadly medieval stage and should look locally made, functional and affordable. Use plain leather, modest iron plates, stone pieces where appropriate, layered cloth, practical belts, boots, gloves and capes.

Keep armour construction simple and compatible with the character budget. Avoid ornate gold, elaborate filigree, crowns, giant spikes, oversized shoulder armour and glowing equipment unless a later specific design is approved.

The equipment layout remains pants, chest, belt, shoulders, gloves, helmet, two rings, necklace and cape. Portraits and scene illustrations need not force every slot into view. Preserve visible facial identity when using a hood or open helmet.

## Portrait presentation

Square portraits use a thin plain rounded-square outline in aged muted copper. Keep it subtle, worn and slightly tarnished, with small rounded corners. No thick decorative bevel, ornate corner fittings or polished gold finish. Quiet dark teal backgrounds support the faces.

## Environment and group scenes

Use attractive fantasy surroundings with restrained detail. A few strong environmental shapes and a modest mysterious landmark are enough. Keep characters prominent and their race features legible.

Group scenes can show two or three characters wearing practical local armour. Their backgrounds must use the same faceted, hand-painted treatment as the portraits. Scenic images do not automatically inherit the portrait UI border.

An illustration's landscape, companions or activity is a visual proposal unless separately agreed as story canon.
